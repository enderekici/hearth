Spotify Connect

Choose the speaker that makes this room yours.

Not connected

1. Create an app at developer.spotify.com/dashboard and copy its Client ID.

2. Add this redirect address in the app settings: http://127.0.0.1:8787/api/spotify/callback

3. On your computer, open a terminal and run ssh -N -L 8787:<PI_HOST>:8787 <PI_USER>@<PI_HOST>. Replace <PI_HOST> and <PI_USER> with your Pi's host and username. Then open http://127.0.0.1:8787/?settings=1 on that computer, save the Client ID, and connect your account.

Spotify opens in the computer's browser through the tunnel. Authorization uses PKCE, so a Client Secret is not needed.

Open Hearth on your computer through the SSH tunnel to finish Spotify authorization. This screen will not open Spotify login.
